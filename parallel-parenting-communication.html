Parallel parenting communication

Less contact. Clearer logistics. Kids out of the middle.

Home / Parallel parenting communication

Parallel parenting reduces direct contact when co-parenting is high-conflict. You still share kids and logistics—you just communicate less often, more carefully, and with tighter boundaries.

Not legal advice. Follow your parenting plan and counsel. Parallel parenting is a communication style, not a court order by itself.

Parallel parenting vs cooperative co-parenting

  • Cooperative — frequent joint decisions, flexible texts, shared problem-solving.
  • Parallel — disengage from personal conflict; stick to written logistics; each household runs its own day-to-day.

If every text becomes a fight, parallel patterns protect kids from the crossfire.

Communication rules that work

  1. Business-only. Schedules, health, school, money—not feelings about the marriage.
  2. Written when possible. Fewer “he said / she said” moments.
  3. One topic per message. No stack of old grievances.
  4. Deadlines for decisions. “Please choose A or B by Thursday noon.”
  5. No bait. Ignore insults; answer only the logistics that matter.

Personal attack → parallel reply

They send

“You’re a terrible parent and everyone knows it. Don’t bother showing up.”

You send

“I’ll be at school at 5:30 for the scheduled handoff. If that needs to change, propose a new time in writing by 3:00 today.”

FAQ

Is parallel parenting giving up?

No. It’s reducing unnecessary contact so kids aren’t in the middle of adult conflict.

Do we still need to talk about emergencies?

Yes—health, safety, and order-required notices. Parallel doesn’t mean silence on urgent child needs.

Is this legal advice?

No—communication guidance only.
