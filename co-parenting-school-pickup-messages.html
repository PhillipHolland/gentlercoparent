School & pickup messages

Logistics texts that keep handoffs boring—in a good way.

Home / Guides / School & pickup messages

School and pickup messages should be dull: times, places, who has the kids. Heat belongs nowhere near the curb.

Not legal advice. Follow your order and school rules.

Templates

Confirm after-school pickup

Confirming I will pick up [Child] today at [time] at [school / door]. Please text only if something changes before then.

Early release / half day

School releases at [time] on [date]. I will pick up [Child] at [location] then. If you are scheduled instead, reply by [deadline] so we do not leave [Child] waiting.

You are running late

I am about [X] minutes behind for the [time] pickup at [location]. New ETA [time]. Sorry for the delay—[Child] will still get [snack / homework plan] as planned.

They are late

We are at [location] for the [time] handoff. Please send an ETA. If you need a permanent time change, propose two options in writing for next week.

Activity after school

[Child] has [activity] from [start]–[end] at [place]. I will drop off / pick up as scheduled. Equipment: [list]. Reply only if that conflicts with the plan.

Hot curb text → calm

Before

“You’re late again and [Child] is crying because of you. This is child neglect.”

After

“We are at the school door for the 3:15 pickup. Please send ETA. For next time, text if you will be more than 10 minutes late so I can plan for [Child].”
