Virtual visitation messages

Video call windows work better when they’re scheduled like exchanges.

Home / Guides / Virtual visitation messages

Virtual parenting time fails when calls are random or become interrogations. Set a window, device, and backup plan—then keep the text thread boring.

Not legal advice. Follow any call rights in your order.

Templates

Confirm call window

Confirming [Child]’s video call tonight at [time] for about [duration] on [FaceTime/Zoom/app]. I’ll have [Child] ready. Please send the link/number if it changed.

Missed call (your side)

We missed the [time] call due to [brief reason]. Can we do [alt time] tonight or tomorrow at [time]? Please confirm.

Missed call (their side)

[Child] was ready at [time] for the scheduled call. Please send a new time for today/tomorrow that works, or confirm tomorrow’s regular window still stands.

Boundary: no adult conflict on calls

Please keep video calls focused on [Child]. Adult logistics stay between us in writing—not through [Child] on the call.

Surveillance vibe → window

After
