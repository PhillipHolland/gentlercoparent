Portals keep the record. Coaches calm the draft. Different jobs.

Home / Co-parenting app vs message coach

Parents often ask whether they need a court co-parenting app or an AI message coach. They’re different jobs. Many families use both.

We don’t replace court-ordered tools. Gentler Coparent helps you write calmer messages you send yourself.

When a portal makes sense

  • Court or counsel recommends documented communication
  • You need shared expense or calendar features both parents use
  • Immutable history is part of your conflict-management plan

When a message coach makes sense

  • You’re stuck on “what do I say?”
  • You write angry drafts you later regret
  • Incoming messages are bait-filled and you need a heat check
  • You want decree-aware Q&A with your order on-device

Using both (common pattern)

  1. Draft or paste in Gentler Coparent (Tone Guardian / Shield / rewrite).
  2. Copy the calm version.
  3. Send in your portal, text, or email—wherever the record needs to live.

FAQ

Do I have to pick one?

No. Portals document. Coaches help you write. Many parents use a coach for tone and a portal for the official thread.

Is Gentler Coparent court-ordered messaging?

No. It is not a shared court messaging system.

Is this legal advice?

No. Product comparison and communication support only.
