Frequently asked questions

Straight answers for parents comparing tools and starting a free trial.

What is Gentler Coparent?

An iOS AI co-parenting coach: Message Shield, Tone Guardian, rewrites, decree-aware answers, and a private journal—without a shared inbox.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. 7 days or 20 messages, whichever comes first. Cancel anytime in App Store settings.

Is this like a court co-parenting messaging app?

No. Portals document shared messages. Gentler Coparent helps you rewrite and tone what you send (or scan incoming heat with Message Shield). Copy drafts into text, email, or the portal you already use.

Does it read my divorce decree?

If you add your order, on-device answers can cite it and open the PDF page. Decree text stays on your device. Not a substitute for reading the signed order or talking to counsel.

Does it give legal advice?

No. Suggestions are communication support only. For legal questions, talk to a qualified attorney.

Where do I download it?

On the App Store for iPhone.

How should I reply in a high-conflict text thread?

Keep it brief, factual, and child-first. Read our free guides: high-conflict messages and text message templates.
