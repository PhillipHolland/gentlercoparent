Frequently asked questions

Straight answers for parents comparing tools and starting a free trial.

What is Gentler Coparent?

An iOS app for high-conflict co-parenting: draft calmer, child-first messages and use optional family context—without a shared inbox or surveillance layer.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. 7 days or 20 messages, whichever comes first. Cancel anytime in App Store settings.

Is this like OurFamilyWizard or AppClose?

No. Those are co-parenting portals for shared messaging and records. Gentler Coparent helps you rewrite the messages you send—clearer tone, firmer boundaries, less fuel for the fight.

Does it give legal advice?

No. Suggestions are communication support only. For legal questions, talk to a qualified attorney.

Where do I download it?

On the App Store for iPhone.
