Medical update messages

Share what the other parent needs to know. Skip the scorekeeping.

Home / Guides / Medical update messages

Health messages should be timely and factual: symptoms, meds, appointments, what you need from the other parent. Not a referendum on who cares more.

Not medical or legal advice. Follow provider instructions and your order’s notice rules.

Templates

Illness notice

[Child] has [symptoms] since [time/date]. Temp [if known]. Home from school today. Meds given: [what/when]. I will update you after [doctor/call/bedtime]. Call if you need anything before then.

Appointment scheduled

[Child] has a [type] appointment on [date] at [time] at [clinic]. I will take [Child] unless you prefer to. Please confirm by [deadline] if you want to attend or take them instead.

New prescription / meds

Provider prescribed [med/dose] for [Child] starting [date]. Instructions: [brief]. Pharmacy: [name]. I will send a photo of the label. Please confirm you received this.

Urgent / ER

Taking [Child] to [ER/urgent care] now for [issue]. Location: [address]. I will text when we know more. You can meet us there if you want.
