Georgia co-parenting messages

Logistics-first messages for GA co-parents.

Home / Guides / Georgia co-parenting messages

If you co-parent in Georgia, the logistics are local—but the messaging skill is universal: brief, factual, child-first, especially when conflict is high.

Not legal advice. Georgia family law is case-specific. This page is about how to write messages, not how a court will rule. Verify schedules and support terms against your signed order and a licensed attorney in Georgia.

What Georgia co-parents text about

  • Custody exchanges and make-up time
  • School and extracurriculars
  • Holiday possession
  • Medical notice
  • Reimbursements

Message rules that travel well

  1. Lead with the kids and logistics—not the marriage.
  2. One topic per message.
  3. When you disagree, point to the written plan—not a legal monologue.
  4. Offer A/B choices with a deadline when you need a decision.
  5. Stop when logistics are clear—even if they keep arguing.

Escalation → logistics

Before

“You always sabotage my time. I’m done being reasonable.”

After

“Per our written schedule, exchange is Friday at 6 pm at [location]. Please confirm. If you read the order differently, reply with that section by Thursday noon.”

Starter templates

Confirm parenting time

Confirming I have [Child] from [start] to [end], exchange at [location] at [time], per our schedule. Text only if something changes.

School pickup

I will pick up [Child] at [school/door] at [time] today. Please message only if the plan changes before then.

Expense share

[Expense] is $[total], due [date] (invoice attached). Your share is $[amount]. Please send via [method] by [date] and reply when sent.
