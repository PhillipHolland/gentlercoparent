What makes Massachusetts co-parent texts different

Massachusetts friction often clusters around school vacation weeks and winter weather—plus tight suburban/city handoffs. The unique angle is vacation-week confirmations and snow-day make-up scripts parents can paste before the week implodes.

How co-parents talk here (language you’ll hear)

  • Parenting plan / parenting time: Confirm the next block with dates.
  • School vacation weeks: MA calendars create predictable text storms—plan early.
  • Winter weather: Propose safer times without blame.
  • Decision-making topics: Separate from logistics when conflict is high.

How early should vacation-week texts go out?

As soon as the school calendar is firm—don’t wait until the Sunday before.

Is this legal advice?

No. Massachusetts parents should verify orders with counsel.
