Right of first refusal messages

Offer the time cleanly. Answer cleanly. No scorekeeping.

Home / Guides / Right of first refusal messages

Some parenting plans include right of first refusal (or similar notice) when a parent needs childcare during their possession. Whether yours does is order-specific—this page is only about how to write the texts.

Not legal advice. Check your decree for hours, exceptions, and notice rules. Decree-aware answers can help you locate language; verify the signed order.

Templates

Offering time (you need coverage)

I need coverage for [Child] on [date] from [start] to [end] during my possession. Per our plan, I’m offering you first refusal. Please reply yes/no by [deadline]. If I don’t hear back, I’ll arrange [babysitter/family] as needed.

Accepting

Yes—I can take [Child] on [date] from [start] to [end]. Exchange at [location] at [time]. I’ll return [Child] at [end time/location] unless we confirm otherwise in writing.

Declining

I can’t take [Child] for [date/time window]. Thanks for the notice—please use your backup care as needed.

Clarify the rule (no lecture)

I want to follow our written first-refusal / childcare notice terms. My reading is [brief]. If you read it differently, please send the section you rely on by [date] so we handle [upcoming date] correctly for [Child].

Weaponized refusal

Before

“You only offer me time when it’s inconvenient—you’re trying to trap me.”

After

“Offering first refusal for Saturday 2–8 pm. Please yes/no by Friday 5 pm. If no response, I’ll use the sitter already lined up.”
