Tone Guardian

Cool the draft while it’s still on your phone—not after it’s in their inbox.

Home / Features / Tone Guardian

You’ve written the angry version. Tone Guardian helps you send the version that protects the kids and your long-term record.

What you get

  • Flags for heat, blame, and escalation risk
  • A calmer, BIFF-style rewrite you can still edit
  • Clear reminder: we don’t message anyone for you

How to use it

  1. Open Tone Guardian from chat starters.
  2. Paste the draft you were about to send.
  3. Tap calm / rewrite. Copy the version you’ll actually send.

When to use Shield vs Tone

  • Message Shield — they sent something hard; you need a response strategy.
  • Tone Guardian — you wrote something hot; you need a cooler send.
