Home / Features / Decree-aware answers

Holidays, support, possession, activities—when the fight is really about the order, generic advice falls short. Add your decree in the app so answers can prefer your language.

What you get

  • Q&A oriented to excerpts from your uploaded order
  • Citation chips (e.g. D1 · p.19) under replies
  • Tap a citation to open the PDF near that page
  • On-device decree text for personal reference in chat

Privacy

Decree PDFs and extracted text stay on your iPhone for this product path. Always verify against the signed order and counsel—apps make mistakes; courts don’t run on chatbots.

What it is not

  • Not a lawyer or legal advice
  • Not a substitute for reading the full decree
  • Not automatic court filing or “proof” by itself
