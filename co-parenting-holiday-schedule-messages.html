Holiday & vacation messages

Confirm the plan in writing. Leave the history out of the thread.

Home / Guides / Holiday & vacation messages

Holidays are when high-conflict co-parenting peaks. Your job is simple on paper: confirm who has the kids, when, and where—per the order or agreed plan.

Not legal advice. Verify dates against your decree. Use decree-aware answers in the app, then double-check the signed order.

Templates

Confirm holiday possession

Per the [holiday / odd-even year] schedule, I will have [Child/Children] from [start day/time] to [end day/time]. Pickup/drop-off at [location]. Please confirm you have the same understanding by [date].

Travel notice

During my [dates] possession, we will be in [city/area]. Emergency contact: [phone]. Flight/hotel details if required by our order: [attach]. [Child] can call/text you at [agreed times].

Request a swap

I am requesting a swap: I would take [dates] and you would take [dates] instead. If that works, please confirm in writing by [deadline]. If not, we will keep the written schedule.

Disagreement on the calendar

I read the order as [brief interpretation] for [holiday]. If you read it differently, please reply with the section you rely on by [date] so we can resolve this before [Child] is in the middle.

Weaponized holiday text

Before

“You’re trying to steal Christmas again like always. The kids hate being with you.”

After

“Per the even-year Christmas schedule, possession starts Dec 24 at 6 pm at [location] and ends Dec 26 at 6 pm. Please confirm the handoff location by Dec 20.”
