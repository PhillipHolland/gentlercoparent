Summer schedule messages

Camps, travel, and long stretches of possession—keep it written and boring.

Home / Guides / Summer schedule messages

Summer multiplies logistics: camps, travel, longer possession blocks, and “who has which week.” Put plans in writing early. Leave personal history out of the thread.

Not legal advice. Match your order’s summer language. Use decree-aware answers as a starting point, then verify the signed plan.

Templates

Propose summer weeks

For summer [year], I propose [Child] with me [dates] and with you [dates], with exchanges at [location] at [time]. Please confirm or send a counter-proposal by [deadline] so we can book camps.

Camp enrollment

[Child] is enrolled in [camp] [dates], [hours], at [place]. Cost $[total] (invoice attached). Your share $[amount] due [date]. Pickup/drop-off plan: [brief].

Travel notice

During my summer possession [dates], we will be in [area]. Emergency phone: [number]. Travel details if required by our order: [attach]. [Child] can call you at [agreed window].

Exchange change request

I am requesting a one-time change: exchange on [date] at [new time/location] instead of [old]. If that works, please confirm by [deadline]. If not, we will keep the written plan.

Summer fight → logistics

Before

“You’re trying to steal the whole summer like last year. Not happening.”

After

“Per our summer schedule, my extended possession is [dates]. Exchange Friday [time] at [location]. Please confirm. If you read the order differently, send that section by Wednesday.”
