Home / Guides / Extracurricular & sports messages

Activities create weekly friction: who drives, who pays, who shows up. Put the plan in writing. Leave coaching critiques and ex-history out of the chat.

Not legal advice. Cost-sharing and decision rights depend on your order.

Templates

Share the season schedule

[Child]’s [sport/activity] schedule for [season]: practices [days/times] at [place]; games posted here: [link]. I will handle transport on my possession days unless we agree otherwise in writing.

Fee / equipment

[Activity] fee is $[total] due [date] (invoice attached). Gear needed: [list] (~$[est]). Your share $[amount] by [date] via [method], unless our order says otherwise.

Who takes them

For [date] practice at [time], I can take [Child] / please take [Child] (you have possession). Drop-off at [place]. I’ll have gear packed: [list].

Conflict with possession

[Event] falls on your possession. If [Child] should attend, please confirm you’ll transport. If not attending, please say so by [deadline] so we don’t leave coaches waiting.

Before

After

“Saturday game is 10 am at [field]. [Child] needs cleats and water. I’ll take them unless you confirm by Friday 5 pm that you will.”
